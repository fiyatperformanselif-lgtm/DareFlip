# DareFlip Support

The offline game does not require a login. To start, choose a mode, add 2–20 names, choose a difficulty, then spin the bottle and choose Truth or Dare. Every card can be skipped without penalty.

- Resume: use Continue on the home screen to return to your locally saved session.
- No repeats: played cards stay out of the draw until the selected pool is exhausted. Changing language must not be presented as fully localized if content is unavailable.
- Sound and vibration: configure them in Settings. Silent mode and system restrictions may suppress feedback.
- Sharing: mobile uses the system share sheet. A browser without file sharing downloads a PNG instead. Canceling sharing does not change the game.
- Ads: optional, native only, and subject to consent and network availability. An unavailable ad never stops the core game. A bonus unlock is granted only after Google confirms an earned reward.
- Privacy: disable ads, reopen Google's privacy options, or reset local data from Settings. Reset permanently removes local progress and favorites.
- Missing translations: some languages may be listed as planned; available content falls back visibly to English. This is not a claim of completed localization.
