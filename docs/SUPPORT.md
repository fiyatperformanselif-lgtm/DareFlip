## Start and play

Choose a mode, enter 2–20 player names for a social game, choose a gender option for each player, and select a difficulty. Prefer not to say is a valid explicit choice. Start the game, spin the bottle and select Truth or Dare.

Private Flirty is a separate mode for exactly two adult partners. Each person makes their own named confirmation that they are at least 18 and want to play. Both must still be willing for every activity. Either can stop or skip without explanation or a penalty. On private cards, “me” and “my” refer to the other partner. Random excludes private Flirty.

## Resume, card editions and language

Continue on the home screen resumes the session saved on this device. Played cards stay out of the matching draw until that pool is exhausted; replay requires an explicit fresh deck. Earlier saved cards and favorites keep their original wording rather than becoming a different card.

An older group save does not automatically become a two-partner private session. Start a new two-person private game with both confirmations for the new private cards. Missing gender details in an older save must be completed; no gender is inferred from a name.

The active English and Turkish card editions are paired. Other listed languages can have partial interfaces and English card fallback. A listed language is not a promise that all cards, legal text or interface strings have been translated.

## Sound, vibration, offline play and sharing

Sound and haptics are configured in Settings. Silent mode or system/device restrictions may suppress feedback. Installed core gameplay works offline. A browser needs a successful first load/cache before offline use; updates, support email, sharing destinations and optional services may need a connection.

Sharing opens the system share sheet or downloads a PNG in a browser. Cancelling sharing does not change the game. Generated card images omit player names and gender choices. Share private-partner content only with both partners' agreement.

A card ID can help identify content issues. Do not send passwords, private answers, intimate photos or other players' personal information.
